# Glossary

**My Script**: a writing system the kid invented and named himself. Each one has its own Glyphs, which he draws. He can make as many as he likes.
_Avoid_: font, cipher, alphabet, code

**World Script**: a real writing system or numeral system (Greek, Hangul, Mayan numerals…) in the Library. He can look at it but not change it.
_Avoid_: alphabet, language, built-in script

**World Character**: one entry on a World Script's page: a letter, syllable or numeral shown big with its name and sound. Tapping it says it aloud.
_Avoid_: Glyph, letter (it may be a syllable or numeral)

**About**: the short description of a World Script: where it's used today, where it came from, and one fun fact.
_Avoid_: blurb, history, info

**Glyph**: the shape he drew for one letter (A–Z), digit (0–9), space or punctuation mark in a My Script. A My Script with only a few Glyphs still works.
_Avoid_: character, symbol, icon, letter (when meaning the drawing)

**Sound**: the few letters he types to say how one of his Glyphs is said out loud, like "kla". The tablet reads it aloud. A Glyph without a Sound is read as the plain letter it stands for.
_Avoid_: name, pronunciation, recording, audio

**Missing Glyph**: a letter in a Message that has no Glyph yet in that My Script. It shows as an empty dotted box.
_Avoid_: blank, placeholder, unknown letter

**Message**: text he wrote in one of his My Scripts, ready to print or send as a link.
_Avoid_: note, cipher text

**Cipher Key**: a chart that goes with a printed Message and shows which Glyph stands for which letter, so someone else can decode it.
_Avoid_: legend, answer key

**Number Explorer**: the section where he counts in the digits of one of his My Scripts and watches how numbers are built. He can change the Digit Count at any time.
_Avoid_: calculator, math quiz

**Digit Count**: how many digits a number system uses, from 2 to 16. Ten is the familiar one. With 5 digits, counting goes past 4 into the next column.
_Avoid_: base, radix (in anything the kid sees, apart from a small caption)

**Stack**: a bundle of blocks that snaps together once there are as many blocks as the Digit Count. A stack of stacks makes the next column. Stacks are how the Number Explorer shows what each digit is worth.
_Avoid_: group, bundle, place value (in anything the kid sees)

**Library**: the section of the app where he browses World Scripts as a reference book.
_Avoid_: catalog, encyclopedia

**Arcade**: the home page that lists every game for the kids, each game with its own tile.
_Avoid_: hub, portal, launcher
